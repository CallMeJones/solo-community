# Solo 0.12.6

Sign in to a Solo account, and be offered the edition you actually have.

## Solo account

- **Settings → Updates, unlock & startup** now has a **Solo account** section
  on every Solo, not only paid ones. **Sign in to your Solo account** links
  this machine in your browser; the tokens are stored on this computer and the
  browser never sees them.
- Signing in changes nothing about how Solo works. Community is the whole
  product with or without an account: nothing here verifies a licence, and no
  feature is gated behind it. All it establishes is whether the account holds
  a paid plan.

## Updates

- The **Edition** choice now offers **Pro** only once the signed-in account
  has it. Until then Solo Community is the only option, with a line saying
  what would change that.
- If Pro was chosen and the account turns out not to have it, Solo puts the
  choice back to Community rather than offering builds the account cannot use.

## Verifying a download

Every package ships a `.sha256` beside it. These installers are **not**
code-signed, so Windows shows its unknown-publisher prompt; compare the
checksum if you want to confirm what you downloaded.

## Install

Windows: press **Check for updates** in Solo, or run
`SoloSetup-0.12.6-x86_64.exe`. It installs per-user, without an administrator
prompt. This release ships for Windows only; Ubuntu stays on 0.12.3.
